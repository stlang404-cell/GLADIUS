# GLADIUS Art Bible — v0.2 final: White Imperial, fully 2D

GLADIUS is a **2D** game with a **white/ivory** interface. Colour and personality come from arena illustrations, piece sets and boards; the UI stays bright, calm and readable.

## Identity
Official logo (original): a Roman gladius on a crimson roundel inside a gold laurel wreath, with the GLADIUS wordmark in Cinzel converted to vector outlines (`src/art/logo.ts`, `npm run build:brand`).
Deliverables in `public/brand/`: horizontal logo (+ tagline, + dark variant), stacked logo, compact crest, square app icon (1024/512/192/180/167/152/120), maskable PWA icons, favicon (SVG, 16–64 px PNG, `favicon.ico`), Windows `gladius.ico` (16–256). SVG masters are self-contained; rasters are transparent where appropriate. The favicon is a simplified sword-on-roundel so it reads at 16–32 px.

## Chess pieces (2D) — v0.4
Hand-authored **vector illustrations** (`src/render/vectorPieces.ts`): every piece is a stack of flat parts (body, gilded trim, crest, engraved ink) in a 100×100 viewBox, lit from the upper left by cylindrical gradients with a gloss stripe and a top-light/bottom-shade overlay, and closed by a firm outline (≈1 px at 40 px squares). Dark pieces carry a thin light halo so they read on dark squares. Designs (shapes) and materials (palettes) are independent. Baked by Chromium to 320 px WebP: `node scripts/bake-sprites.mjs pieces [setIds]` → `public/pieces/<folder>/<w|b><p|n|b|r|q|k>.webp` (≈84–90 KB per set; budget ≤ 120 KB). Set metadata (id, name, description, unlock rule, folder) lives in `src/board2d/pieceSets.ts`; `Board.tsx` derives `PIECE_STYLES` / `pieceSrc` from it, and `normalizePieceStyle` maps stored/legacy ids.

**King vs queen (every set).** The queen wears a *wide, open* crown — a five-point pearl coronet (`coronet()`) or the Praetorian radiate fan — wider than her slender, waisted body. The king wears a *closed, domed* crown under a single tall finial — the cross-orb (`crossOrb()`), or the gilded aquila for the Praetorians — on a broader, heavier body, and stands tallest. Fan vs spire is the recognition cue at 32 px; colour is never needed.
- **Legio** (`classical`, primary, always) — ivory vs ebony, gold: legionary-galea pawn with red crest, crenellated tower rook with gate and a gilded *tabula* engraved **SPQR**, horse with red legionary crest along the mane and gilded bridle, augur mitre bishop with slit and gold finial, pearl-coronet queen with a garnet, laurel-banded closed crown king with arches and cross-orb.
- **Senate Marble** (`marble`, arena 4) — Carrara vs Nero Marquina with veining, silver fittings; architectural forms: column-drum pawn with sphere, Senate-gate rook (arched passage under a crenellated attic, so it reads as a rook), sculpted horse bust with carved mane locks, Vestal-flame brazier bishop, Ionic queen on a waisted trumpet shaft with a silver pearl coronet, heavy fluted Corinthian king under a laurel-banded dome and cross-orb.
- **Praetorian Guard** (`imperial`, arena 10) — burnished steel vs oxblood lacquer, gilded: legionary pawn in a galea with a transverse centurion crest and cheek guards (classic pawn silhouette — no longer a shield block), gate-tower rook with portcullis and a gilded shield boss, armoured warhorse with chanfron, upright crest and peytral, flamen's apex bishop, radiate-crown queen (nine rays), helm-crowned king carrying a compact aquila with raised wings.
Rules: silhouettes differ by type at 32 px (pawn smallest; king tallest with a single vertical finial; queen widest crown); light/dark sides differ in value (not just hue) and pass grayscale and deuteranopia checks; no Unicode glyphs. **Quality gate** (re-run after any change): every piece at 240 px, then 90/40/32 px on light and dark squares of several boards; a mid-game position on all 25 boards (24 signature + Eternal Laurel) at 90, 40 and 32 px squares per set, with markers; grayscale and deuteranopia sheets of all boards × sets; a 32 px "blink test" sheet with only K/Q pairs (both colours, light and dark squares of every board); and true 32 px rasters magnified ×3. Look at every sheet.

## Boards (2D)
Twenty-four signature boards — one per arena in the 24-arena empire — plus Eternal Laurel, painted once to canvas textures (veined marble, sandstone grain, travertine pores, granite speckle, mosaic tesserae, brushed bronze/gold, obsidian with gold flecks, laurel inlay, and since v0.3 wood planks, cross-laid papyrus and porphyry speckle) with a material frame, gold fillet and Cinzel coordinates. Original twelve (ids unchanged; arena = 2 × old index): Colosseum Sandstone 0, Legion Bronze 2, Citadel Granite 4, Gladiator's Travertine 6, Ivory Marble 8, Praetorian Crimson 10, Senate Mosaic 12, Imperial Gold 14, Flavian Arena 16, Palatine Amethyst 18, Caesars' Verdigris 20, Caesar's Obsidian 23. New: Ludus Oak 1, Frontier Pine 3, Ostia Harbour Blue 5, Aqueduct Travertine 7, Thermae Teal Marble 9, Circus Red Granite 11, Alexandria Papyrus 13, Capitoline Porphyry 15, Pantheon Grey Granite 17, Tibur Garden Green 19, Domus Aurea Gilded 21, Victoria Alabaster 22. Eternal Laurel (23) is earned at the Eternal Throne. Board ids are stored in saves and never change; `signatureTheme(0..23)` falls back to the nearest lower arena. Each theme names the piece set shown for "match each board" (`pieces`). Textures stay restrained (mosaic tesserae kept low-contrast) so pieces always lead. Markers are built to read on every board: last move = gold wash + thin gold inset edge (from-square lighter); selection = gold glow with a two-tone (gold + white) inset ring; legal dots = charcoal with a faint light rim; capture = crimson ring with white edges, inside the square; check = pulsing red glow with a red inset edge (the pulse is the non-colour cue); hint = blue; keyboard cursor = blue inset.

## Motion (2D) — v0.4
Timing lives in `src/board2d/animation.ts` (`PRESET_MS`, `moveDuration`, `planMove`); the board (`src/ui/components/Board.tsx`) turns a move into frame-driven tracks on DOM sprites. Total per move: **Majestic 950 ms** (default) · Balanced 450 · Fast 200 · Custom 150–1200 (slider, default 480); **Reduce motion** (setting) or the OS `prefers-reduced-motion` = instant.
**Opponent / engine / spectated moves** (anything the board did not send itself):
1. *Pre-highlight* (20 % of the total, 60–220 ms; Majestic 190): the piece rises slightly and glows gold on its square, with a gold ring on the origin; the origin is already marked.
2. *Glide* (Majestic 627 ms): ease-in-out along a straight ground track (monotone, no regressions) with a gentle lift (knights arc higher), lift and pose kept in a separate transform component.
3. *Capture*: approach to contact, gold impact ring, the victim tumbles away and is fully gone by the landing; the attacker pushes in. En passant hits the pawn on its real square.
4. *Settle* (14 %, ≤150 ms; Majestic 133): weighted squash; the destination highlight fades in and a thin gold landing ring flashes, so the completed move reads as from + to. Last-move marks then persist.
The move's sound plays when the piece lands (GameScreen delays the cue by `landingMs`).
**The player's own moves** never wait: no pre-highlight, the glide starts on the next frame and lasts 75 % of the preset (Majestic 712 ms); a dragged-and-dropped piece only slides from the drop point into its square (60–140 ms).
**Special moves**: castling — the king glides while the rook hops over him in concert (starting at 30 % of the glide, landing together); promotion (all four pieces, by dialog or auto-queen) — the pawn arrives as a pawn, shrinks into a golden flare and the chosen piece pops out, within the same total; check — the check square fades in on landing and the king flashes red twice (the CSS pulse continues); checkmate — the mate lands, a golden laurel flare marks the mating square and the beaten king topples and greys (120 ms after landing, ≥600 ms) before the ceremony; resignation — the king topples at once.
**Engineering**: chess state is authoritative and outside; sprite identities follow the FEN, and any new position first *finishes* everything in flight (timeline, CSS/WAAPI effects, transient poses) so the DOM always converges to the FEN — no teleports, ghosts, duplicates or lingering captured pieces. Any input (tap, drag, key, double-click) finishes the animation instantly and is accepted; nothing is queued, so unmounting, navigating away, reloading, undoing or changing speed mid-move is safe. **Verified** by `npm run test:anim` (`scripts/anim-check.mjs`): frame sampling in Chromium on the board lab (`boardlab.html`) and on the real GameScreen in a production build — convergence, monotone glide, duration bounds, pre-highlight on AI moves only, all special moves, rapid input with instant engine replies, undo, speed change, reduced motion, unmount/reload mid-animation, touch taps on a phone viewport, spectating.

## Arenas
Twenty-four original 2D illustrations (`src/art/arenaBackdrop.ts`) with crests (`src/art/arenaCrest.ts`), each its own place, palette and light; the board sits on a white stage in front of the arena. Budgets: < 90 KB per backdrop, no filters/text/images/external refs, deterministic, per-arena id prefixes.
v0.4 refinements: Circus Maximus — crowd texture dimmed toward the stone with faint seat rows, spina cleared (lap-counter frame and near statues removed; dolphins kept low at the near end); Harbour of Ostia — dressed-stone quay (block courses, lit kerb, jointed sea face, mooring rings), amphorae with rims, loop handles, painted bands and sheen stacked two deep in racks, planked crates, a fallen amphora and a grain sack; Aqueduct Heights — the big cumulus and an eagle sit over the centre so the phone crop's top strip has a subject; Iron Citadel — Romanised: square towers with low tiled roofs on posts (no spires), principia pediment, vexilla and an aquila instead of pennants, iron-bound timber gates under voussoir arches instead of portcullises; Praetorian Bastion — string courses, plinths, buttress piers, wall and tower return faces, corbel tables, voussoir rings and torch light pools give the walls depth; Hall of Caesars — statues re-carved in contrapposto with cuirass, pteruges and paludamentum (or toga), fold lines and far-side shade; Golden House — Fourth-style frescoes (dark frieze, framed landscape pinax, candelabra, garland swags, navy dado with a gold meander).
Crests: the arena numeral sits on a wide ribbon at 1.4–1.7× the old size with a dark outline (cream on the bronze tier) so it reads at 40 px.
